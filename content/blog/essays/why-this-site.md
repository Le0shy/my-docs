This site is my writing hub. It has three shelves:

1. **Blog** — essays and notes on anything.
2. **Knowledge Base** — paper notes, concepts, systems and industry history in database systems, data management and cloud warehouses.
3. **Reviews** — marks and reviews of music, films and books.

The goal is simple: write things down, retrieve and brush up on them later, and give my own views.
